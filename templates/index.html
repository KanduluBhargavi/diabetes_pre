Medical Terminology

Key Terms Explained

Understand the biomarkers used in diabetes screening.

HbA1c
Glycated Haemoglobin
Average blood glucose over 2–3 months. Gold standard for T2DM diagnosis. Formed by non-enzymatic glycation of haemoglobin.
Normal <5.7%Pre 5.7–6.4%DM ≥6.5%
FPG
Fasting Plasma Glucose
Blood glucose after 8hr fast. Reflects hepatic glucose output and insulin sensitivity. ADA 2024 key screening marker.
<100IFG 100–125DM ≥126 mg/dL
BMI
Body Mass Index
Weight-to-height ratio estimating body fat. High BMI drives insulin resistance via TNF-α/IL-6 inflammatory pathways.
Normal <25OW 25–29.9Obese ≥30
T2DM
Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Metabolic disorder with chronic hyperglycaemia due to progressive insulin secretory defect and insulin resistance. 90% of all diabetes cases.
Prediabetes reversibleT2DM manageable
IR
Insulin Resistance
Reduced cellular sensitivity to insulin. Central T2DM mechanism. Assessed via HOMA-IR = (fasting insulin × glucose) / 405.
HOMA-IR <2 Normal>3 Resistant
ADA
American Diabetes Assoc.
Sets international clinical standards for diabetes diagnosis and management. This tool follows ADA Standards of Care 2024.
Standards 2024
